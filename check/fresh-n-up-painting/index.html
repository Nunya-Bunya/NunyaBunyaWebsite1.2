What I found

Each one is something I could observe from the outside, not a guess about your business.

The footer still says 2023

The copyright line at the bottom of your site reads 2023, which is 3 years ago.

People read a stale date as “this business might not be running anymore”, especially when they're deciding who to call first.

How to fix itChange the year in the footer, or better, have it set itself automatically so it never goes stale again.

There's no contact form and no tap-to-call link

I couldn't find a contact or quote form on the page, and the phone number isn't set up as a link a phone can dial.

That leaves copying the number out by hand for anyone who found you through the site. Every extra step loses a share of the people who were ready to enquire.

How to fix itTwo small changes: make the phone number a tap-to-call link, and add a short form (name, phone, what they need). Short forms out-perform long ones.

There's very little text for Google to read

The main page has roughly 887 characters of readable text. For comparison, a page that ranks well for a trade usually carries several thousand, covering services, areas served and common questions.

Google can only rank you for words that appear on your site. With this little text there's not much for it to match a search against, so you're mostly found by people who already know your name.

How to fix itThe highest-value additions are usually a page per service you offer, and a page per area you cover. Written for customers first, not for Google.

What's already working

Worth saying, since it's easy to only hear the problems.

Your site loads over a secure connection (HTTPS), so no browser warnings.
The site is set up to resize properly on phones.
Mobile load speed is in a healthy range on Google's PageSpeed test.
