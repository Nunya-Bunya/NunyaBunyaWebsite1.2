What I found

Each one is something I could observe from the outside, not a guess about your business.

Your site loads without a secure connection

Your site is served over HTTP rather than HTTPS. The practical effect is that Chrome and Safari show a “Not secure” warning in the address bar before anyone reads a word of your page.

Most people don't know what the warning means, only that it looks wrong. On a quote page it reads as “don't type your details here”.

How to fix itMost hosts issue a free SSL certificate (that's the thing that turns HTTP into HTTPS) in a couple of clicks. Search your host's help docs for “free SSL” or “Let's Encrypt”. Usually under an hour, often ten minutes.

There's no contact or quote form

I couldn't find a form on the page, so enquiries rest on the phone alone.

Plenty of people won't ring, especially outside business hours. A form catches the ones who'd rather type, and it catches them at 9pm.

How to fix itThree or four fields is enough: name, phone, and what they need. Anything longer costs you responses.
