The shape of it

Four phases
over a year.

Your audit will be specific to your business. This is the general order, and the reasoning behind it: start where a mistake is cheap, finish where the money is.

Phase 1
Mo 1–2

The boring wins

Inbox triage, meeting notes, turning one recording into a week of posts. Nothing here touches a customer, so a mistake costs you nothing. You get time back in the first fortnight, and you find out whether working this way suits you before anything important depends on it.

Phase 2
Mo 3–5

The paperwork

Quotes, proposals, invoices, follow-up documents. The work you do after the client says yes. It's repetitive, it's slow, and it's the same shape every time, which is exactly what a machine is good at. You review before anything goes out.

Phase 3
Mo 6–9

The money layer

Answering enquiries fast, qualifying them, and following up properly. This is where the real return is, because every service business quietly loses work to slow replies. It goes third because by now you trust the system and we know what your customers actually ask.

Phase 4
Mo 10–12

Running it yourself

Handover. Documentation, the logins, and the training so your team can change things without calling me. If you still need me after twelve months, something went wrong in the first eleven.
