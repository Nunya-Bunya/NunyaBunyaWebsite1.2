How the sprint works

Simple by design.

A 15-minute video call to talk through your space, your menu or product, and the shots that matter most. 50% deposit starts the sprint. You get a plain-English shot list with framing, angles and examples for every clip.

You capture the footage on a phone or camera, on your own time, around your service so you keep trading. We stay on call to review takes and guide the shots. No crew in your way, no date to schedule around.
