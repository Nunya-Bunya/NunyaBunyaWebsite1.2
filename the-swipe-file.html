Questions Anticipated

Asked & answered.

Where do the swipes come from?
Real ads, posts, and emails I see converting in the wild — across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and my agency clients' campaigns. Sources never include identifying details. You get the structure, the hook, the pattern. Not someone else's brand name.
Is this just AI-generated content?
No. AI is great at making a lot of mediocre content fast. The Swipe File is the opposite: a small number of swipes that actually worked, curated by a human who's looked at thousands. AI is a tool I use; it's not the product.
What if my industry isn't represented?
A good swipe is structural. The hook for a café reel is the hook for a personal trainer reel is the hook for a tax accountant reel — you swap the noun. Most members run businesses I've never written specifically for.
How do I get the drop?
Every Friday morning (AEST) you get an email with a link to that week's drop folder. It lives in Google Drive — written swipes as text files, reel scripts as PDFs, subject lines as a single doc. Nothing to install.
Can I cancel?
Anytime, one click, no email-us-to-cancel nonsense. Stripe handles it. If you cancel mid-month you keep access until your next billing date.
Will The Swipe File ever go up in price?
Yes — almost certainly. When it does, founding members lock in $27/mo forever. The longer you wait, the more you'll pay.
Why $27?
Cheap enough to be a no-brainer for one good month. Expensive enough that I take it seriously and you actually use it. Free swipe files exist. They're free for a reason.
