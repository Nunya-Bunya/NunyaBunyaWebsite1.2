09Questions

Questions tradies ask

Q1How much do I need to spend on ads?

At least about $30 a day, paid to Meta on your own card. Past that it depends on how many jobs you want, and your Full Diary Plan works that daily budget out for your trade and postcodes. It's an estimate to start from, and the first months show the real numbers.

Q2What does it cost all up?

$2,000 setup when you sign, then $1,200 a month starting 30 days after your ads go live. Book by 24 December and your first month is free. Your ad spend is separate and goes straight to Meta, with nothing added. Prices are AUD and no GST is added.

Q3How soon will the phone ring? Do you guarantee jobs?

Ads usually go live about two weeks after you say yes, and enquiries go to you the moment they come in. I won't promise a number or a date, because nobody can honestly guarantee jobs from ads that haven't run in your area yet. The first month is the platform learning who responds. I judge it on booked jobs, read monthly, and I'll tell you straight if it isn't paying for itself.

Q4Is the Full Diary Plan really free?

Yes. It's ready within 2 business days and it's yours to keep. If you'd rather run the ads yourself, it tells you where to start.

Q5Do I need a website first?

No. I build a page for each service you advertise, so the ad and the page say the same thing. If you already have a site, it stays as it is.

Q6Do I get a say in the ads?

You tell me the jobs you want, send the photos and set the budget. I write and run the ads, and you get a heads-up whenever one changes and why.

Q7Is there a lock-in?

Three months, then month to month. The ad account is in your name and the card is yours, so if we stop, the account, the ads and the data stay with you.

Q8I'm a licensed builder in Queensland. Does that change anything?

Yes. Every ad and page shows your licensed name, licence number and that you're licensed under the QBCC Act, as the QBCC requires. That's set up before anything runs.
