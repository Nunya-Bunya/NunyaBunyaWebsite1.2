03How It Works

How It Runs

  1. 01A free call

    We talk about the jobs you want more of, where you work and what a good month looks like for you.

  2. 02Setup

    I set up access to your ad account, build a landing page for each service, and write three to five different ads from your photos. You set the budget, and you see every ad before it runs.

  3. 03Live

    Ads usually go live about two weeks after you say yes.

  4. 04Every Monday

    I check spend, enquiries and cost per enquiry, and ask you what happened to each lead. No changes that day.

  5. 05Every second week

    One edit day: I swap out the ads that aren’t pulling their weight and leave the rest alone, because constant fiddling resets the platform’s learning.

  6. 06Every month

    A plain-English report: what you spent, what came in, and what I’m changing next.

05Questions

Questions Trades Ask

Q1Do I need a website first?

No. I build a landing page for each service you advertise, so the ad and the page say the same thing. If you already have a site, it stays as it is.

Q2Who pays for the ads?

You do, straight to Meta, on your own card in your own ad account. My fee is separate. If we ever stop working together, the account, the data and the ads stay with you.

Q3How much do I need to spend?

You set the daily budget. Small budgets work if they're set up for it: one campaign, three to five different ads, and patience. I currently run a renovation campaign at $30 a day.

Q4How soon will I get enquiries?

I can't promise a number or a date. Ads usually go live about two weeks after you say yes, and the first month is mostly the platform learning who responds. I judge it on booked site visits and jobs, read monthly, not on one week.

Q5Where do the leads go?

Each enquiry is emailed to you within seconds of someone filling in the form, with their details and the ad they came from, so you can call them back while they're still keen.

Q6I'm a licensed builder in Queensland. Does that change anything?

Yes. Every ad and landing page shows your licensed name, licence number and that you're licensed under the QBCC Act, as the QBCC requires. I set that up before anything runs.

Q7Do you only work in Brisbane?

I work remotely with businesses anywhere in Australia, and in the US. Your ads target the postcodes you actually service, not a circle on a map.

Q8Is there a lock-in?

Three months, because that's how long the numbers take to mean anything. After that it's month to month.
