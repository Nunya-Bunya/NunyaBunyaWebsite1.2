Straight answers

The questions you're
actually asking.

If it's open source, why pay at all?

You don't have to — every tool inside is free, and you can wire it together yourself if you've got the time and the patience. The membership pays for the installer that does it in one command, the templates that work out of the box, and the monthly drop that tells you what to actually do with it. You're buying the assembly and the direction, not the software.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can copy and paste one command, you can stand up the stack. The whole point is that the install does the part agencies charge a month of labour for.

What does it actually replace?

Your CRM, your email marketing platform, your analytics, your link tracking, your booking tool, and the automation glue between them — the rented SaaS stack that quietly adds up to thousands a month. All of it, on your own server.

It's early. What am I really buying?

A founding seat in something being built in the open, at a price locked for life, with every new module handed to you the day it ships and a direct say in what comes next. If you'd rather wait until it's polished, that's fair — you'll just pay more and have no say.

What if I cancel?

You keep everything you've installed. It's open source and it's on your box — cancelling stops the new drops and updates, it doesn't take anything away. No hostage situation. That's the whole idea.

🛡 Own It, Or It's Not Working

Everything the Founder System installs runs on your own server, under your own account — open source, no watermarks, no lock-in. Cancel any time and you keep every tool and every automation. The membership earns its place every month or you walk with everything you built. That's the deal.
