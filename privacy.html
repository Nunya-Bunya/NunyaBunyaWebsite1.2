Privacy policy.

Last updated 27 July 2026

Nunya Bunya Pty Ltd (ABN 48 890 965 530) runs this website and provides remote marketing services. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be impressive.

The short version: we collect what you send us in a form or an email, plus basic analytics about how the site is used. We do not sell your data to anyone, ever. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

What we collect

  • Things you tell us. Your name, email address, phone number, business details, and whatever else you type into a contact form, quiz, or email to us.
  • Analytics. Pages visited, roughly where in the world you are, what kind of device you used, and which site sent you here. This is collected by Matomo, which we host on our own server rather than sending to a third party.
  • Payment information. If you buy something, payment is handled by Stripe. Card numbers go to Stripe directly. We never see or store them. We keep the record of what was purchased and when.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to you when you get in touch.
  • To deliver what you bought.
  • To send you email you asked to receive. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and it works.
  • To understand which pages are useful so we can improve the site.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the services we need to run the business:

  • Stripe for payments.
  • Google Workspace for email.
  • Our own self-hosted tools for email sending and customer records, which run on a server we control rather than a third-party SaaS platform.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

The site uses cookies for basic functionality and for Matomo analytics. If your browser is set to send a "Do Not Track" signal, Matomo respects it. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site will still work.

Some pages carry a Meta (Facebook) pixel so we can measure whether our advertising works. You can control this in your Facebook ad settings.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and contact records are kept while there is a live or likely business relationship, and removed on request. Financial records are kept for five years because Australian tax law requires it. Analytics data is retained in aggregate.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it if it is wrong, or delete it. Email ben@nunyabunya.com and we will respond within 30 days. There is a separate page explaining how to request deletion.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Security

We use encrypted connections, keep our systems patched, and limit access to client data to those who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your information.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Email ben@nunyabunya.com.
