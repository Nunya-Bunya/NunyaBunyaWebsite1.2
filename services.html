08FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What's the difference between Bedrock and a retainer?
Bedrock is a one-time setup: I build your website, landing page, email sequence, booking system, and marketing foundation. Retainers (NB Onyx, Tanzanite, Black Opal) are ongoing monthly marketing: I manage your social media, ads, email, and content every single month.
What if I'm not happy with the work?
Tell me, and I fix it. You set the budget, and I send you a heads-up with every change, so nothing about your ads is a surprise.
Do I own the work you create?
100%. Everything I create, whether logos, websites, content or graphics, is yours to keep forever. You get all source files, all logins, all assets. I never hold your work hostage.
What happens after the 3-month minimum?
You can cancel anytime with no penalties. No long-term contracts, no cancellation fees. You're never locked in.
Can I start with Bedrock and upgrade to a retainer?
Yes, and that's the ideal path. I build your foundation with Bedrock, then manage and grow it on a monthly retainer. All your assets, data, and systems carry straight over.
How fast will I see results?
A Bedrock build takes 4 to 6 weeks. With paid ads, you see what every dollar did from the first week: I send you spend, leads and cost per lead every week. SEO results typically take 60-90 days for significant rankings improvement. I'll set realistic expectations based on your specific situation.
What's included in the free 15-minute audit call?
It's a 15-minute call where I review your current online presence (website, social media, SEO, ads) and give you honest, actionable feedback. No pitch, no pressure. You walk away with a clear picture of what's working and what needs fixing, whether or not you end up working with me.
