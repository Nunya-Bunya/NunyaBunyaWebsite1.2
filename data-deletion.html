Deleting your data.

Last updated 27 July 2026

This page explains how to ask Nunya Bunya to delete data we hold about you, and what happens after you ask. It also covers our internal Meta application, NB Dashboard.

How to request deletion

Email ben@nunyabunya.com with the subject line Data deletion request. Tell us:

  • The email address, phone number, or account you used with us.
  • Roughly when you were in contact with us, if you remember.

You do not need to give a reason. We will confirm we received your request within 5 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

If you contacted us from an email address you no longer control, say so in your message and we will find another way to confirm it is you before deleting anything.

What gets deleted

When you ask us to delete your data, we remove:

  • Your contact record and any email history in our mailing system.
  • Enquiry and form submissions you sent us through this website.
  • Any notes or files we created about you as a prospective or former client.

What we may have to keep

Some records we are legally required to hold onto, even after a deletion request:

  • Invoices and payment records. Australian tax law requires businesses to keep financial records for five years. Payments are processed by Stripe and are subject to Stripe's own retention rules.
  • Records relating to a live legal or contractual matter, until that matter is finished.

We will tell you if any of this applies to your request, and what is being kept.

NB Dashboard (our Meta application)

NB Dashboard is an internal tool. It is used by Nunya Bunya to publish posts to Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that Nunya Bunya owns or manages on behalf of clients.

It is not a consumer app. Members of the public do not sign into it, and it does not collect or store personal data about visitors, followers, or anyone who interacts with those pages. The only credentials it holds are our own access tokens for our own pages.

If you believe NB Dashboard holds data about you, email ben@nunyabunya.com and we will investigate and respond within 30 days.

Facebook and Instagram data

If you want to remove data held by Meta rather than by us, that is handled through your own Facebook or Instagram account settings. You can remove our app's access to your account at any time in the Business Integrations section of your Facebook settings.

Questions

Email ben@nunyabunya.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
