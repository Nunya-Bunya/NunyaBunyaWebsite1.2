05FAQ

Questions you're probably asking.

What if I'm not happy with the work?
30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, full refund. Beyond that, every deliverable goes through a review process. Nothing ships without your sign-off, and revisions are unlimited.
Do I own everything you create?
100%. Every file, every asset, every login, every piece of content. I never hold your work hostage. If you leave, you take everything with you.
How long until I see results?
Website builds take 3-4 weeks. If you're on a retainer, SEO results typically start showing at 60-90 days. Paid ads and social media can show movement within the first month.
What's the difference between the one-time packages and the retainer?
One-time packages like Bedrock build your foundation. Retainers keep it running every month: social media, ads, email campaigns, content, and strategy. Most clients start with a foundation build, then move to a retainer when they're ready.
Can I cancel the retainer?
After the 3-month minimum, cancel anytime with no penalties. I don't lock you in because I don't need to.
